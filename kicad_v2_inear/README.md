# Haven v2 BTE in-ear unit: schematic

The small board in the in-ear unit: SPH0641 PDM mic (the primary mic; SELECT low = DMIC0) (bottom port, needs a hole through the
board), 100 nF decoupling, a 33 Ω series resistor on the mic's data line, six cable pads and two
speaker pads. Target outline about 6 × 6 mm, 2-layer, 0.6 mm.

The design notes, cable harness table and decisions are in `../kicad_v2_pod/README.md`. Both
schematics are generated by `tools/gen_v2_bte_schematic.py`.

For the 2-wire variant (pod mic only), leave the mic, C1 and R1 off, or skip this board and wire the speaker straight to cable W5/W6.

## Layout (`haven_v2_inear.kicad_pcb`)

Generated by `tools/gen_v2_inear_pcb.py`. The script places, routes, pours and fills, and writes the
design rules into the `.kicad_pro`. Results: DRC 0 violations, 0 unconnected, and schematic parity 0
(`kicad-cli pcb drc --schematic-parity --severity-all`).

- 6 × 6 mm, 2-layer, 0.6 mm, corner radius 0.8. The outline matches haven-hardware
  `mechanical_cad/v2_bte/board_inear_outline.dxf`. One board fits both ears.
- **Bottom (driver side):** U13 SPH0641LU4H-1. It is bottom-port (datasheet / DigiKey "Port
  Location: Bottom"), so its port sits on a plated 0.4 mm hole at the board centre. That hole is
  pad 3 (GND), added to the footprint and lined up with the shell's mic hole. The driver pads
  TP1/TP2 sit on the same face as the speaker.
- **Top (lateral side):** cable pads W1..W6, with C1 next to W1 and R1 in the DATA line. Nothing
  is placed within 1.4 mm of the port (`GASKET_KEEPOUT` rule area), so a 0.5 mm foam gasket can
  seal the board to the shell's mic hole.
- Rules (JLCPCB 2-layer standard): 0.15 mm tracks, 0.127 mm clearance, 0.3 / 0.55 mm vias,
  0.25 mm copper to edge. GND is poured on both layers.

Before ordering, check:
- The port hole and the paste on the four GND pads around it, against the Knowles land-pattern
  drawing. Solder must not flow into the port. If it might, trim the paste on the inner corners.
- The SPK and cable wire pads (0.8 mm) are for hand soldering 6-wire harness conductors under
  a microscope. Vias are in the pads, which is fine for hand soldering.
