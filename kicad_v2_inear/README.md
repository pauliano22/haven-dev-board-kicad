# Haven v2 BTE in-ear unit: schematic

The small board in the in-ear unit: SPH0641 PDM mic (the primary mic; SELECT low = DMIC0) (bottom port, needs a hole through the
board), 100 nF decoupling, a 33 Ω series resistor on the mic's data line, six cable pads and two
speaker pads. Target outline about 6 × 6 mm, 2-layer, 0.6 mm.

The design notes, cable harness table and decisions are in `../kicad_v2_pod/README.md`. Both
schematics are generated by `tools/gen_v2_bte_schematic.py`.

For the 2-wire variant (pod mic only), leave the mic, C1 and R1 off, or skip this board and wire the speaker straight to cable W5/W6.
