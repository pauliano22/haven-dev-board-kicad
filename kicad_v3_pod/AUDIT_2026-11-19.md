# v3 pod: datasheet audit, 2026-11-19

**Why this exists.** Earlier reviews were open-ended ("look for mistakes"). Each one found something new, so a clean
result never meant much. This audit is a fixed checklist: every pin of every chip, every support part, every footprint
that has a maker's drawing, and every firmware pin, each against its primary source. It ends when the list is done.
Items that only a built board can answer are listed at the bottom, not hidden.

Board checked: `haven_v3_pod.kicad_pcb` as of `v3_pod_FINAL6_review_fixes_2026-11-18`. Netlist from
`kicad-cli sch export netlist`. Datasheets in `~/.cache/haven_datasheets/` (TI sheets downloaded today to
`audit_dl/`). Fixes: `tools/v3_patches/audit_fix.py`; result snapshot `v3_pod_FINAL7_audit_2026-11-19`.

## Noted, not changed (judgement calls, measure on the bench)

- **Buck ripple.** MODE is tied low (automatic). Below 90 mA the buck stays in hysteretic mode, ripple up to 80 mV p-p
  (PS v1.5, BUCK electrical specification: IPWMTHRES 90 mA, VOUTBRIPPLE_HYST 80 mV p-p); the pod never reaches 90 mA. The codec rejects supply ripple by 85-90 dB (Rev C, PSRR), so this should
  not be audible. Measure +1V8 ripple and the speaker noise floor on the first board.
- **Output capacitance.** Nordic gives 6-20 uF effective at VOUTB. With the codec rail on, about 30-35 uF hangs on it
  (estimate after DC-bias derating). Fine in hysteretic mode by reasoning, not by Nordic's figure. Watch start-up.
- **Supply ceiling.** The codec's absolute maximum (1.98 V) equals its operating maximum; VOUTB is 1.8 V -2 % / +8 %.
  Typical boards sit near 1.8 V. Measure +1V8 on the first board before enabling the codec.

  handedness were checked; U2 and U3 pad sizes were checked 2026-11-18).
- Soldering of the 0.35 mm-pitch audio chip and the LGA radio module (X-ray requested in the build instructions).
- Radio range with the spring pins 1 mm from the antenna keep-out.
- The ADAU1860 driver has never run on a real chip. Firmware can be changed after the board exists; the board cannot.
- Shell fit and the cell's real size.
